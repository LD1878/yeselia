Residence Casco Antiguo

€1,850,000

A restored residence within the old town walls: historic fabric, contemporary finish, absolute quiet.

Description

Behind an unassuming façade in Marbella’s Casco Antiguo lies a fully restored three-bedroom residence. Original structural elements have been retained and refined; services, insulation and climate systems are entirely new. The plan is simple and elegant, with a double-height living volume and a private roof terrace above the rooftops. This is a property for those who prefer the texture of the old town to resort anonymity, without sacrificing modern comfort or legal clarity of title.

Location

The old town is Marbella’s historic core: pedestrian streets, orange trees and a pace that differs from the coastal strip. Restaurants, galleries and the Orange Square are on the doorstep. Parking is available in nearby private facilities.
