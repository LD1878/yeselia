La Zagaleta · Benahavís

Villa La Zagaleta South

€18,900,000

A south-facing La Zagaleta estate of eight suites, with a long plot, mountain air and complete privacy.

Description

This eight-suite estate occupies a south-facing plot of more than six thousand square metres within La Zagaleta. The principal house is arranged as a sequence of pavilions rather than a single block, so that family, guests and staff occupy the land without collision. Eight bedroom suites, a formal dining room, a library, a cinema, spa and indoor pool, and a separate staff wing. Outside: a tennis court, a long pool, and gardens that have had a decade to establish. Club access is subject to La Zagaleta’s rules. A house of this scale is not frequently available; the legal and club position has been examined with corresponding care.

Location

La Zagaleta sits in the hills above Benahavís, a private gated estate with its own golf courses, security and restricted access. Marbella and Estepona are both within a short drive; the estate is intentionally removed from both.

Features

  • La Zagaleta club setting
  • Eight bedroom suites
  • Tennis court
  • Indoor and outdoor pools
  • Staff wing
  • Cinema and spa
  • Garage for eight cars
  • Helipad access within the estate
