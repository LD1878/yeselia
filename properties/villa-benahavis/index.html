La Zagaleta · Benahavís

Villa La Zagaleta

€12,500,000

A substantial estate within La Zagaleta, offering scale, privacy and uncompromising specification.

Description

Within the exclusive gated community of La Zagaleta, this villa presents a rare combination of acreage and architectural discipline. The principal house is organised around a central courtyard, with formal and informal wings that allow large households to live without intrusion. Seven bedroom suites, multiple reception rooms, a cinema, spa and staff quarters are arranged with clear circulation. Outside, the plot supports mature gardens, a tennis court and a infinity edge pool oriented toward the mountains. Ownership here is not merely residential; it is membership of one of Europe’s most private residential environments.

Location

La Zagaleta is a private residential club above Benahavís, known for its security, golf courses and limited supply. Access is controlled. Marbella and Estepona are both within a short drive, while the estate itself remains intentionally removed.

Features

  • La Zagaleta membership included subject to club rules
  • Tennis court
  • Spa and indoor pool
  • Cinema room
  • Staff wing
  • Helipad access within the estate
  • Extensive landscaped gardens
  • Garage for six vehicles
