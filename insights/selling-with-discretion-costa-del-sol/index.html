Selling with discretion on the Costa del Sol

Not every owner wants a public listing. How confidential marketing works when privacy is non-negotiable.

18 September 2025 · 5 min read

Some properties should never appear on open portals. Owners with security concerns, public profiles or simply a preference for quiet sales need a different approach.

Confidential marketing means controlled exposure. The property is presented only to pre-qualified buyers who have demonstrated capacity and genuine intent. Viewings are scheduled, accompanied and limited. Details that identify the address may be withheld until a serious stage is reached.

Preparation still matters. Discretion is not an excuse for incomplete documentation. Title, licences, community status and a realistic valuation should be in order before any introduction is made. A prepared file shortens the buyer’s diligence and protects the seller’s negotiating position.

Pricing under privacy. Off-market does not mean underpriced. It means the audience is smaller and better informed. Accurate valuation against recent comparable transactions remains essential.

At Yeselia & Co., seller mandates can be structured as fully confidential, limited circulation, or conventional open marketing. The choice is yours. The standard of care is the same.
