Difference

Why work with a lawyer-agent

On the Costa del Sol, beautiful presentation is common. Clean legal status is not always. When your adviser is also a qualified lawyer, risk is identified earlier, documents are read with a different eye, and commercial decisions rest on solid ground.

You are not managing parallel relationships with an agent and a separate legal team who meet only at crisis points. One practice. One standard. One accountable process.

Process

How an acquisition proceeds

A clear sequence designed for high-net-worth buyers who value time and certainty.

  1. 01

    Define the brief

    Location, scale, budget, timeline and lifestyle requirements. We establish what is essential and what is flexible.

  2. 02

    Curated shortlist

    You receive only properties that meet the brief. Off-market opportunities are included where appropriate and available.

  3. 03

    Accompanied viewings

    Viewings are organised efficiently. We attend, advise and filter in real time so your attention is reserved for serious candidates.

  4. 04

    Legal diligence

    Before reservation or private contract, title, planning, licences, community status and tax implications are examined.

  5. 05

    Negotiation and contract

    Terms are negotiated with legal precision. Deposit structures, conditions and timelines are drafted to protect your position.

  6. 06

    Completion

    Notary completion, funds and post-completion formalities are coordinated as a single, orderly sequence.
